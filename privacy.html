Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

SASE Practice Hub PH ("we", "our") operates the website sase-practice-hub-ph.vercel.app and the SASE Practice Hub PH Facebook Page. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Facebook Page interactions: When you comment on our Page posts, we receive your public comment text and name via Facebook's webhook system to provide quiz answer replies.
  • Order information: When you order the MSU-SASE Reviewer, we collect your name and GCash reference number to verify payment and deliver your PDF.
  • Website usage: Basic, anonymized analytics (page views) via Vercel's built-in analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To reply to your quiz comments with correct answers.
  • To verify payments and deliver purchased digital products.
  • To improve our educational content.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Retention

Order records are kept to prevent duplicate reference-number use. Quiz comment replies are processed in real time and not stored beyond operational logs. You may request deletion of your data by messaging our Facebook Page.

4. Facebook Data

Our Facebook integration only accesses public comment data on our own Page posts, as permitted by Facebook's Platform Terms. We do not access your private messages, friends list, or personal profile data beyond what is needed to reply to your public comments.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, message us at SASE Practice Hub PH on Facebook.
