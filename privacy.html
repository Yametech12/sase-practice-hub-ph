Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

SASE Practice Hub PH ("we", "our") operates this website, the mock exam portal at /exam, and the SASE Practice Hub PH Facebook Page. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Facebook Page interactions: When you comment on our Page posts, we receive your public comment text and name via Facebook's webhook system to provide quiz answer replies.
  • Order information: When you order the MSU-SASE Reviewer or the Civil Service Exam Reviewer, we collect your name, your Facebook / Messenger name, your order reference, and your GCash reference number to verify payment and deliver your PDF.
  • Portal access: When you use the mock exam portal, we collect and store:
    • Portal credential (hashed): your reviewer password (SASE) or CSE7 access code (Civil Service) is verified against a stored one-way hash. The credential itself is not kept in readable form.
    • Device IDs: a random device identifier your browser generates, used to enforce the up-to-3-devices-per-credential limit.
    • Exam answers and results: the answers you submit, your scores, and your answer-review history, so you can see your results and review every answer.
    • Session tokens: a temporary sign-in token kept in your browser while you use the portal, so you do not have to re-enter your password on every page.
  • Website usage: Basic, anonymized analytics (page views) via Vercel's built-in analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To reply to your quiz comments with correct answers.
  • To verify payments and deliver purchased digital products.
  • To run the mock exam portal: sign you in, enforce the device limit, save your answers, grade your exams, and show your results and answer reviews.
  • To improve our educational content.

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our pages load the following third-party services, which may collect their own data (such as your IP address, device and browser information, and pages viewed) under their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel hosting & analytics, the site is hosted on Vercel, which provides basic anonymized usage analytics.
  • Facebook, Page comments and messages are processed through Facebook's platform (see Section 5).

4. Data Retention

  • Order records (name, Messenger name, order reference, GCash reference) are kept to prevent duplicate reference-number use and to handle support requests.
  • Portal data (password hash, device IDs, exam answers, results) is kept for as long as your portal access is active, so your results and answer reviews remain available. Exam answers and results may be kept after an exam is completed so you can review them later.
  • Session tokens are temporary: they expire, are cleared when you log out, and are stored only in your browser.
  • Quiz comment replies are processed in real time and not stored beyond operational logs.

You may request deletion of your data by messaging our Facebook Page (see Section 6). Deleting portal data removes your stored answers and results, and cannot be undone.

5. Facebook Data

Our Facebook integration only accesses public comment data on our own Page posts, as permitted by Facebook's Platform Terms. We do not access your private messages, friends list, or personal profile data beyond what is needed to reply to your public comments. Order messages you send us via Messenger are used only to verify payment and deliver your purchase.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, message us at SASE Practice Hub PH on Facebook.
