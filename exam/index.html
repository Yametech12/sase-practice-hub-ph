How it works

  1. Pick one answer per question (A, B, C or D). You can change it any time before you submit.
  2. Your answers save automatically to the server, so a refresh or dropped connection won't lose them.
  3. The timer keeps running if you close the page. Come back to Your Exams and tap Resume before time runs out.
  4. Use Flag and the Grid to mark questions to revisit and jump to any question. Skipped questions score no points.
  5. Submit when you're done. If time runs out, your saved answers are submitted for you.
  6. You can't retake an exam. Submitting or giving up uses your one attempt.
